# Amazon Results No Sponsored Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 2, 2026

Amazon Results No Sponsored is a browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Safari on Mac. It hides sponsored placements on Amazon.com search result pages.

The extension examines Amazon's page markup locally to identify sponsored placements. It does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data or browsing activity. It makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics, accounts, or third-party SDKs.

Your normal use of Amazon.com remains subject to Amazon's own privacy practices. If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL.
