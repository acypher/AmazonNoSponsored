## Description

See Amazon.com search results without sponsored placements. This Safari extension hides sponsored product cards, carousels, video ads, brand banners, and other marked sponsored sections on Amazon.com search result pages.

It works with Amazon's usual sort options and with other extensions that reorder the results. Sponsored items are hidden in the page while organic products remain visible.

The extension runs only on Amazon.com search result pages. It has no account, analytics, or data collection. If Amazon changes how it labels sponsored placements, some ads may reappear until the extension is updated.

Independent extension; not affiliated with Amazon.

## App Review notes

Open the app and use its button to open Safari Extensions settings. Enable “Amazon Results No Sponsored” and allow it on Amazon.com. Search Amazon.com for coffee beans. Sponsored product cards and banners will be hidden; ordinary results remain visible. No login or test account is required to use the extension.
